Musk's New X Profile Photo Mocks Biden's Dark Brandon Meme

  • Authored by: Jenel Treza Albuquerque
  • Updated: Jul 22, 2024, 01:30 IST

Elon Musk updated his X profile photo following Joe Biden’s withdrawal from the 2024 presidential race. The new image features Musk with glowing blue laser eyes, seemingly mocking Biden's "Dark Brandon" meme, which was characterized by beaming red laser eyes and widely used during Biden's reelection campaign.

Elon Musk changes his X profile photo.

Photo : Twitter
follow usfollow us

Elon Musk updated his X profile photo moments after Joe Biden announced his withdrawal from the 2024 presidential race. Furthermore, Biden also endorsed VP Kamala Harris as his replacement. The new image features Musk with glowing blue laser eyes, set against a red and blue background with an American flag. The image seemingly mocks Biden's "Dark Brandon" meme, which was also characterized by beaming red laser eyes and was widely used during Biden's reelection campaign.

Musk's profile update comes amid a surge of political activity on his page. In his recent tweets, Musk expressed support for a potential Trump/Vance ticket. It was a reference to former President Donald Trump and J.D. Vance, the Republican Senate candidate. Musk tweeted, “My smartest friends, including those living in the San Francisco Bay Area who have been lifelong Dems, are excited about Trump/Vance.” He further declared, “I believe in an America that maximizes individual freedom and merit,” suggesting that the Republican Party now embodies these values more than the Democratic Party.

Musk's reaction, in light of Biden's departure from the race, was notably dismissive. He reacted with a laughing emoji to Biden’s announcement. In response to a tweet from @KanekoaTheGreat, who criticized the Democratic elite and corporate media for pressuring Biden out of the race, Musk responded with “Exactly.” The comment aligns with Musk's known political inclinations.

The "Dark Brandon” meme plays on Biden’s tough-talking persona in some online circles. It has been used as a comical spin on his leadership style and political decisions.

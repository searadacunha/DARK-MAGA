"Dark MAGA" has been a magnum opus tier experiment in timeline hyperstition, I'll explain the background on where the phrase came from in a short thread here: 🧵
In January of 22' someone in a GC made a joke that a picture of Yugi from Yugi-oh was "Dark MAGA" and we all started riffing on it in other group chats with photoshopped pictures of "gothic" Donald Trump and silly joke posts about what exactly "Dark MAGA" was as opposed to MAGA.
It wasn't particularly serious, just some anons clowning around on the TL. The type of libs with no sense of humor who lurk this particular side of twitter as "researchers" took it seriously and wrote some lame hit piece on it: isdglobal.org/isd-in-the-new…
So we had some fun with it, people started making laser eye PFPs for fun and making them for other people, including some politicians and the like. This only encouraged the hit pieces taking what was just a joke way too seriously.

motherjones.com/politics/2022/…
Some staffer in the Biden Admin must have caught wind of it because "Dark Brandon" memes started appearing, since the left lacks any original thoughts of its own. At this point we all kind of decided they'd made it cringe and we packed it up for business as usual on the bird app.
Fast forward to this year, and some of us in a GC once again created something by just riffing unseriously on a joke about our wives which spawned "Wifejak".

One of our guys who does crypto stuff made a wifejak meme coin, and it actually made some money off the hype.
We've experimented with "hyperstitioning" ideas onto the timeline before out of boredom, but we started to realize that crypto may have a role to play in that. So we concoted the idea of the "Culture Coin", creating a financial incentive for a certain form of posting.
So we sort of beta tested this with a prototype coin of a political nature on Solana, and started posting memes and attracting jeets into the telegram who were now obsessively posting political material in an effort to do the needful and make the green line go up.
That coin did ok, and we made and learned from a lot of mistakes along the way about how to manage a meme coin. But the goal as a bunch of RWers on twitter was "Can we financially incentivize pro trump political content being posted by otherwise apolitical crypto apes?"
And so @kikisknees started @DarkMagaCoin, and started making PFPs for other people to use with the red filter and the laser eyes, with the idea that we'd refurbish the Dark MAGA aesthetic from 2022 this time.
What we didn't count on was that two days later, Trump would survive an assassination attempt at his Butler, PA rally. So our timing was good for the sentiment of the Dark MAGA aesthetic. Tons of people started using the PFPs we were making, poor Kiki was working overtime.
What we didn't expect was who would start using the PFPs we made. Guys like Andreesen, Utah Senator @basedmikelee, and then to our surprise, Elon Musk himself. The coin mooned pretty hard, and a few days later Musk changed back to his regular PFP.
Hit pieces were written, of course, because the left seems intensely interested with whatever we've got going on in this part of Twitter.

dailydot.com/debug/dark-mag…
We were kind of hoping we could convince Elon to change his PFP back, so we kept with that, kept making memes for fun, we had some people in the GC who Elon follows so he had visibility, a lot of accounts close to Elon started making news posts using our DMAGA pics.
We knew we were getting somewhere, because the Dems were appropriating the aesthetic to write crappy hit pieces about Trumps "Dark Turn" but it all looked cool enough to just be an album cover instead. We were reaching the egregore.

Then when Trump returned to Butler to hold a rally in the same place he had been shot, Musk gave a shoutout that he "wasn't just MAGA, but Dark MAGA" with the black hats that are being dubbed "Dark MAGA Hats" and now even Jon Stewart is kvetching about it.
Now the actual campaign is using the phrase, and in their own cringe but adorable boomer way saying "Elon Musk *is* Dark MAGA!" like it's his superhero name like Bruce Wayne is Batman or something, but it's the nature of the Boomer to act this way so I'll give them a pass.
This has also had pretty much exactly the result you would expect with regards to $dmaga, so make of that what you will.

But the original goal of the lark that we started in 2022 has more or less been a success, we've hyperstitioned a meme into the mainstream.
And that was really the goal all along, from a stupid Yugioh joke to figuring out if we can get an idea and an aesthetic onto the lips of Senators, a President, and one of the richest men in the world.

And if we could make teeming masses of crypto guys support an election.
Just to see if we could.

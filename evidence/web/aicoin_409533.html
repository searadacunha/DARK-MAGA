Biden dropped out, but DMAGA is on fire? Musk switched to laser eyes to support Trump. What's the background of this dark MEME coin?

CN
PANews
Follow
2 years ago
AI summarizes in 5 seconds.

Author: Frank, PANews

No one could have expected that after Biden announced his withdrawal from the election, the meme coin that became popular in the MEME world was the dark-themed Trump meme with laser eyes, known as DMAGA (Dark MAGA).

Twitter's Laser Eye Trend: The "New Aesthetics" of Trump Supporters

On July 21, U.S. President Biden announced his withdrawal from the 2024 presidential election, stating that he would fully support and endorse Vice President Harris as the Democratic presidential nominee. Along with this news, there was a collapse in Biden-related meme coins. According to incomplete statistics, within a few hours of Biden's announcement, tokens such as BIDEN and BODEN experienced short-term declines of over 60%. In contrast, the corresponding meme coins for the new presidential candidate Harris saw a short-term increase of over 2.7 times.

However, what seemed unexpected to everyone was that amidst this new surge in traffic, the most lively phenomenon was not the several high-market-value tokens related to Trump. Instead, a token named DMAGA experienced a nearly hundredfold surge in value, reaching a market value of nearly 83 million U.S. dollars, after Elon Musk changed his profile picture to one with laser eyes.

Biden's withdrawal seemed to particularly excite Musk. After the news broke, Musk posted multiple related contents on Twitter regarding this topic. As early as July 19, Musk had tweeted, "They're about to take Biden fishing soon," and the news of his withdrawal seemed to confirm his prediction. Subsequently, Musk changed his personal Twitter profile picture to one with a dark, laser-eyed style.

According to KJ Crypto, a member of the DarkMAGA team, the image used by Musk was created by the DarkMAGA community as a PFP image. Currently, several key opinion leaders (KOL) have also changed their profile pictures to this laser-eyed style. The Dark MAGA community continues to create corresponding images for various celebrities. Users can also upload photos on the official website to create their own dark-themed avatars.

Different from the previous red laser eyes associated with the Biden camp, the popular laser eyes on July 22 had a red background with blue laser eyes. On social media, this seemed to become a distinctive symbol of the two competing camps. Although the Dark MAGA style appears to portray Trump as a kind of dictator with a dark image, in fact, it represents a new political culture within the Trump camp. According to Tim Squirrell, Director of ISD Communication and Editing Department, in an interview, "Dark MAGA" is becoming a new aesthetic advocated by supporters and more extreme political groups. "Rather than being a political slogan, it is more of a cultural gene. It is a post-alternative right-wing aesthetic that promotes a dystopian, Terminator-like image of a dictatorial version of Trump. In some images, Trump Tower is completely painted black, and you can see the former president with blue laser eyes piercing through the screen."

Surge of Dark-themed MEME Coins, Musk Calls X a MEME Goldmine

With the success of DMAGA, an increasing number of dark-themed MEME tokens have emerged. PANews has briefly compiled a list of six high-market-value dark-themed MEME tokens on Solana. Among them, DMAGA still has the highest increase, reaching 92 times, while DARKELON's performance is equally impressive, with a maximum increase of over 57 times. LASER EYES saw a maximum increase of about 47 times, while the remaining dark-themed tokens, Dark Harris, DARK JESUS, and DARK TRUMP, all have market values of less than a million U.S. dollars and have experienced rapid fluctuations, failing to be considered true "gold mines."

The success of DMAGA has also allowed many early investors to achieve high profits. According to on-chain analyst @ai_9684xtpa, six days ago, the address GXZvX…ZG2Ra purchased DMAGA tokens worth 48.5 SOL, and as of now, their cumulative return rate has reached 7688%, with overall profits exceeding 500,000 U.S. dollars. Of course, such investments may rely on luck to achieve, and when you choose the wrong direction, you may also pay a heavy price. For example, on July 22, a Polymarket user, AnonBidenBull, lost 2 million U.S. dollars after betting on Biden winning the Democratic presidential nomination, election, and general election.

Overall, this dark-themed trend may continue with the support of Trump's campaign and individuals like Musk. Musk seems to be well aware of the token surge brought about by this MEME culture, as he commented on a tweet about Harris possibly being on the vice presidential list, saying, "X is now a MEME goldmine."
